# Public website content notes

## Positioning

HiQuant Consulting is positioned as a cross-disciplinary consulting firm for scientific, regulated, and technology-focused organizations. The language intentionally avoids implying that the business is a healthcare provider or that the public website is a regulated client portal.

## Service architecture

The Phase 2 homepage groups the planned offerings into six clear entry points:

1. Pharma and biotech consulting
2. Quality and GMP
3. Analytical and laboratory
4. IT and technology strategy
5. Data and automation
6. Digital transformation

## Calls to action

Public calls to action currently lead to the homepage contact section. The final secure inquiry action remains visibly unavailable until the Phase 3 form and its validation are implemented. This prevents the site from advertising a workflow that does not yet exist.

## Legal-page status

`privacy.html` and `terms.html` are practical launch drafts based on the planned website behavior. Before production launch, the business owner should:

- Add verified business contact details.
- Confirm actual analytics, hosting, database, email, and anti-spam providers.
- Confirm retention practices and applicable jurisdiction.
- Arrange legal review appropriate to the business and its operating locations.

The pages do not claim that the public website is HIPAA-compliant and instruct visitors not to submit confidential, patient-identifiable, clinical, trade-secret, or other regulated information.
