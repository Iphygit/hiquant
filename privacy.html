Privacy notice

This notice explains how HiQuant Consulting LLC intends to handle information submitted through this website.

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Do not submit sensitive regulated information.

Please do not include confidential, proprietary, trade-secret, patient-identifiable, clinical, or other regulated information in an initial inquiry. Appropriate secure channels can be arranged after an engagement is established.

1. Scope

This notice applies to information collected through the HiQuant Consulting LLC public website, including future consulting inquiry and appointment-request forms. It does not describe information handled under a separately executed client agreement.

2. Information we may collect

When the website forms are enabled, information may include:

  • Your name, business email address, telephone number, and preferred contact method.
  • Your company, job title, industry, and requested consulting category.
  • A high-level description of your business need, timing, duration, and approximate budget range.
  • Requested meeting type, date, time, time zone, and non-sensitive notes.
  • Basic technical information generated by website and service providers for security and reliable operation.

3. How information may be used

Information submitted through the website may be used to review and respond to inquiries, assess whether HiQuant can support a request, coordinate an introductory meeting, maintain business records, protect the website, and comply with applicable obligations.

Submitting an inquiry does not create a client or advisory relationship.

4. Sharing and service providers

HiQuant does not intend to sell personal information submitted through this website. Information may be processed by service providers used to host the website, store submissions, support authentication, prevent abuse, or operate the business. Those providers handle information under their own terms and privacy practices.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights or safety, investigate misuse, or complete a business reorganization.

5. Retention and security

Inquiry information should be kept only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, legitimate business records, and applicable legal requirements. HiQuant uses reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you previously submitted, subject to identity verification and any information HiQuant must retain. Available rights may vary by location.

7. Children’s privacy

This business-to-business website is not directed to children, and HiQuant does not knowingly seek personal information from children through it.

8. Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated as the website and business practices evolve. The date above identifies the latest revision.

9. Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent through the website’s consultation inquiry page. Live transmission remains disabled until the secure backend integration is completed. Verified business contact details will be added before production launch.
